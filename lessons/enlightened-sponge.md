About 144 proofs were witnessed through a delegated helper while one family of 39 proofs was on hold. Only afterwards did reading the author command show that it validates the whole proposed estate before writing either file, so no admission can be published while any active post-baseline proof is unadmitted. The receipts are valid and keep their value, but the order hid the real blocker until after the expensive step, and the operator learned it late. Correction: before starting a large evidence-producing batch whose results land through an all-or-nothing publisher, read the publisher's preflight and confirm every precondition can be met, or report the blocker first along with the cost of proceeding anyway.
