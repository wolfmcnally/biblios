The phase sketch was elaborated at entry after kickoff-evidence init had captured it, and its deliverables and acceptance list were amended again after the plan review and after each code critique. The run's implementation, both critiques, the primary decision and a green gate were all recorded before the acceptance validator refused with 'declared authority changed; re-review in a fresh evidence run', because a captured authority is immutable for its run and nothing rebinds it. The repair cost a park, a corrective run and a second gate. Remedy: elaborate the phase file completely before init; when a review finding genuinely requires an authority edit, make it, park and recapture at once rather than continuing to the gate; and have the planning resource say that review-driven acceptance amendments are an authority change, not an ordinary plan revision.
